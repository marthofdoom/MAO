1.0.4
- Extended Synthesis (the +30s buff-duration perk) now shows the extended
  duration on the flask's item card, so what you see is what the drink applies.
- Extended Synthesis is now stat-fortify only by default (Fortify/Resist and the
  like); it no longer lengthens restore-over-time healing. New MCM toggle
  "Extend all buffs" (Field Kit > Perks, default off) restores the old reach.
- MCM renamed to "marth Alchemy Overhaul".

1.0.3
- The Field Kit power is the only opener that ships bound; the always-on "K"
  keyboard fallback now ships DISABLED (opt back in via iOpenHotkey). Opening the
  kit via the power or an alchemy station is unchanged.

1.0.2
- Economy rebuild. Flask cost now derives from a potion's gold value on a log
  scale anchored to your load order's cheapest real potions, so quality ladders
  price consistently (a stronger potion never costs less than a weaker one) on
  vanilla, Requiem, or a heavy overhaul list alike.
- Guaranteed tiers per the design: above-anchor potions require Catalyst; the top
  rungs of a potion's own family require Apex. Rare essence no longer accrues as
  fast as common.
- REQUIRES A SYNTHESIS RE-RUN when updating from 1.0.1 or earlier: the patcher now
  emits potion-family and quality-anchor data the DLL reads. Without it the mod
  runs a reduced fallback (logged as DEGRADED). Two MCM sliders were renamed, so
  custom threshold values reset to defaults.

Existing essence balances are never rescaled across any of these updates.
